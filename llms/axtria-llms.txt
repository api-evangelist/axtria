# Axtria

> Axtria is an AI-first cloud software and data analytics company for the life sciences
> industry, founded in 2010 and headquartered in Berkeley Heights, New Jersey. Its cloud
> products help pharmaceutical, biotech, medical device, animal health and consumer health
> commercial teams turn data into targeting, segmentation, incentive compensation, field
> force and brand decisions. Axtria reports 3,600+ employees serving 100+ life sciences
> companies, including 16+ of the top 20 pharmaceutical enterprises.

## API surface

Axtria publishes **no public API, no developer portal and no machine-readable API contract**.
As of 2026-07-31 the following were probed and missed on every resolvable Axtria host
(`www.axtria.com`, `axtria.com`, `docs.axtria.com`): `/openapi.json`, `/swagger.json`,
`/llms.txt`, `/.well-known/security.txt`, `/.well-known/openid-configuration`,
`/.well-known/oauth-authorization-server`, `/.well-known/api-catalog`,
`/.well-known/ai-plugin.json`, `/.well-known/agent-card.json`, `/.well-known/agent.json`.
`api.axtria.com`, `developer.axtria.com`, `developers.axtria.com`, `portal.axtria.com`,
`support.axtria.com` and `status.axtria.com` do not resolve.

Axtria runs a real product documentation host at `https://docs.axtria.com/`, but it is
**HTTP Basic-auth gated to customers** (401 on every path), so no documentation, reference
or contract is publicly readable. Platform integration — Veeva CRM / Veeva Vault CRM,
Salesforce, Marketo and 20+ structured and unstructured life sciences data sources over
SFTP and APIs — is delivered under enterprise contract, not through self-service.

No hosted MCP server, no A2A agent card, no published SDK on npm / PyPI / Maven Central /
NuGet / RubyGems / pkg.go.dev, and no `axtria` GitHub organization were found.

- [Axtria InsightsMAx.ai](https://www.axtria.com/cloud-products/axtria-insightsmax-cloud-analytics/): agentic AI platform — agents, apps and enterprise APIs (contract-gated)
- [Axtria SalesIQ](https://www.axtria.com/cloud-products/axtria-salesiq-ai-driven-sales-planning-and-operations/): sales planning, territory alignment and incentive compensation
- [Axtria MarketingIQ](https://www.axtria.com/cloud-products/axtria-marketingiq-marketing-analytics/): marketing analytics and omnichannel measurement
- [Axtria CustomerIQ](https://www.axtria.com/cloud-products/axtria-customeriq-omnichannel-customer-engagement/): omnichannel customer engagement
