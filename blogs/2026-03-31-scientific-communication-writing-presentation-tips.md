Why Is Scientific Communication Important? As researchers, we spend weeks, months, and even years building a strong collective body of scientific knowledge. The value of our discoveries and insights can impact several pharma-related stakeholders, including hospitals and other institutions, healthcare professionals, manufacturers, and most importantly, patients.
